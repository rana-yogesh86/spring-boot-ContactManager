Account

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign up" in the top navigation, fill in your name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters, and submit the form. You'll be able to log in right away.

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?". Enter your account email and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.

Can I change my email address?

Yes — open your profile page and update the email field under Account details, then save your changes.

Contacts

How do I add a new contact?

Click "+ Add contact" from the navbar or dashboard, fill in the details, and save. It'll immediately appear in your contacts list.

How do I search for a contact?

Use the search bar on the "All contacts" page to filter by name, phone number, or email as you type.

Can I undo a deleted contact?

Deletions are currently permanent, so double-check before confirming. We recommend exporting your contacts periodically if this matters to you.

Security

Is my data private?

Yes — your contacts are only visible to your account. Other users can't see or access your data.

How do I log out of all devices?

From your profile page, use the "Log out" option. If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately, which will end other active sessions.
